About Me

"Real learning isn't what gets remembered — it's what becomes a habit."

I design human-centered, AI-integrated learning experiences — technology should make training faster, not colder. It started in 2008, in front of engineering students, with a question I couldn't shake: why did so little of what I taught actually stick? Fifteen years and 10+ industries later, that question hasn't changed. What's changed is my answer to it. And the standard I hold every project to is simple: if it doesn't change what someone can actually do, the design isn't finished.
